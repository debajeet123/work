About

Highly motivated geophysicist with extensive expertise in seismic processing, ambient noise tomography, and inverse modeling. Experienced in applying probabilistic and unsupervised machine learning (including deep reinforcement learning, GANs, and variational autoencoders) to complex geophysical inverse problems. Proficient in Python, C/C++, Fortran, MATLAB, and GPU programming (CUDA, MPI) for high-performance computing. Proven track record in developing robust algorithms for surface wave tomography, beamforming (MUSIC), full waveform inversion, and stochastic Bayesian modeling — all essential for advanced seismic data analytics.
