About Me

I am a geophysicist specializing in seismic imaging, ambient noise tomography, and machine learning. My PhD from Baylor University focused on developing computational models for subsurface exploration. I thrive in interdisciplinary environments where high-performance computing meets earth science. My research spans GPU-accelerated modeling, DAS, and AI-enhanced inversion workflows.

Research Timeline

2020–2021

Adjoint-free FWI using Hamiltonian operator theory, CUDA/MPI for elastic modeling.

2021–2022

TensorFlow pipeline for ambient noise tomography with the MUSIC algorithm.

2022

Fieldwork across Texas and Nevada using DAS and microgravity data.

2023

Implemented GANs and physics-informed neural networks for inversion problems.

2024–Present

Building browser-native seismic visualization tools using WebGL/Three.js.
